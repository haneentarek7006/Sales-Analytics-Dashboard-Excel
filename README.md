# 📊 Sales Analytics Dashboard

An interactive dashboard providing key insights into overall sales performance, monthly revenue trends, regional distribution, and top-performing representatives.

![Sales Analytics Dashboard](Screenshot%202026-08-25%20222539.png)

## 🔑 Key Metrics & Highlights
- Total Revenue: $438,636 across 195 total orders.
- Top Performing Region: North Region ($144,060 in sales).
- Top Sales Representative: Ahmed Ali ($105,442 in sales).
- Highest City Sales: BAHAWA ($67,181).

## 📈 Visualizations Included
- Sales Trend Wise Month: Line chart tracking monthly performance, peaking in December ($67k) and June ($56k).
- Sales by Representative: Bar chart showcasing sales volume per representative.
- Sales by Region: Breakdown across North, East, South, and West regions.
- Top 5 Customers: Horizontal bar chart highlighting top revenue-generating clients.
- Deal Count & Payment Types: Analysis of transaction sizes and preferred payment channels (Cash, Check, Credit Card).

## 🛠️ Tools Used
- Data Visualization & Analytics: Excel (Pivot Tables, Power Query, Dynamic Slicers)
